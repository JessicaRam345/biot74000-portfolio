## My Capstone Project

What are the antimicrobial effects of nisin and rosmarinic acid at various concentrations against *Listeria monocytogenes* planktonic cells? *Listeria monocytogenes* is a pathogen that can form biofilms and is a major issue in the food industry as it can be resistant to sanitizers and increase risk of food contamination. The scope of this study is to assess the effects of these natural antimicrobials and see whether nisin and rosmarinic acid, when used alone or in combination with each other, can reduce bacterial growth.

## The Bioinformatics Component

RStudio was used to help visualize the growth curves of *Listeria monocytogenes* when combined with specific treatments and compare them with each other. This visualization helped determine which antimicrobial treatment caused the greatest antimicrobial effect against the planktonic cells. This tool was the perfect choice to visualize the data, as it is capable of processing and visualizing the large datasets that were obtained during this project.

Growth curves of nisin treatments, rosmarinic acid treatments, and combination treatments (nisin + rosmarinic acid at various concentrations) were produced from this tool. This analysis compared the growth curves and concluded that nisin + 12 mM rosmarinic acid showed the greatest inhibition across all the treatments tested. This combination treatment resulted in a delayed bacterial growth. It did not completely prevent the growth of *Listeria monoytogenes* but it did inhibit it for a few hours and extended the lag phase. In the future, assessing this antimicrobial for longer time period could help determine what concentration is capable of completely preventing the growth of *Listeria monocytogenes*.
